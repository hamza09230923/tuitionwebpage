# Brag Plan: MySchola 30s Launch Film

## Angle
MySchola turns GCSE support into a visible weekly rhythm. The film uses continuous product transformations: the homepage learning plan becomes subject cards, subject cards become the timetable, and the timetable becomes the student dashboard.

## Brand
Use the source brand system: ink `#071a33`, navy `#0d2b52`, blue `#145dff`, dark blue `#0c43bd`, ice `#f5f8fd`, line `#dbe6f2`, slate `#5f7084`, cyan `#63c7f5`, and support green `#10a56a` only for positive/support states. Use the real project graduation-cap mark from `public/favicon.svg` and the MySchola wordmark.

## Duration / format
30 seconds, 1920x1080 landscape.

## Storyboard
1. 0–4s: Logo assembles from the source mark and wordmark on deep navy. Hook: `GCSE success is built week by week.` Camera pushes into the real homepage.
2. 4–9s: Homepage hero headline reveals word-by-word. Learning-plan card floats forward; weekly progress bar draws on. The card folds into the next scene.
3. 9–14s: The card transforms through Maths, English and Science course panels. Panels slide and align into one learning system.
4. 14–20s: Subject panels morph into the real weekly timetable. Lessons populate across the week. A pointer selects a Maths lesson and the details panel expands.
5. 20–26s: Timetable slides continuously into the student dashboard. Join Zoom, recordings and homework cards rise into focus. Homework becomes feedback received; a progress line draws across the interface.
6. 26–30s: Dashboard layers compress into the logo. Final copy: `Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades.` Then `Book a free consultation` and `No card required.`

## Voiceover
Use Kokoro `bf_emma` or `bm_george`, professional British delivery.

“GCSE progress is built one week at a time. MySchola brings live small-group tuition, clear routines and expert support into one place. Maths, English and Science. Timetabled lessons, recordings, homework and feedback that keep students moving forward. For parents, progress becomes clearer too. MySchola. Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. Book a free consultation.”

## Audio
Use `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at a restrained level, ducked under narration. Add subtle clicks on selection, soft glass accents on transformations and one quiet logo impact. Both music and narration must be audible in the final MP4.
