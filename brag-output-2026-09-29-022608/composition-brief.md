# Hyperframes Composition Brief: MySchola

Create a 30-second highly animated, premium Apple-inspired product launch film for MySchola using real source-derived UI. The animation must be continuous and purposeful throughout: camera pushes, parallax, 3D depth, masks, morph-like transitions, spring easing and UI microinteractions. Do not make static slides.

## Output
- 1920x1080 landscape
- 30 seconds
- `brag-output-2026-09-29-022608/brag.mp4`

## Source files
Use `src/Home.jsx`, `src/index.css`, `src/pages/Courses.jsx`, `src/pages/Timetable.jsx`, `src/pages/StudentDashboard.jsx`, `src/pages/Recordings.jsx`, `src/pages/Homework.jsx`, and `src/pages/Booking.jsx` as the source of truth for copy and UI patterns.

## Brand
Ink `#071a33`; navy `#0d2b52`; blue `#145dff`; dark blue `#0c43bd`; ice `#f5f8fd`; line `#dbe6f2`; slate `#5f7084`; cyan `#63c7f5`; green `#10a56a` only for support / positive states. Use `assets/brand/myschola-mark.svg` and the real MySchola wordmark treatment. No invented logo or palette.

## Motion contract
Animate every scene with layered movement. Transform the homepage learning-plan card into subject cards, subject cards into the timetable, and timetable into the dashboard. Use selected lesson expansion, dashboard card rise-ins, feedback status change and progress-line drawing. Keep text readable and transitions related to the product story. Avoid stock imagery, generic AI students, particles, blobs, glitches and unrelated graphics.

## Audio
Use `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` and a British Kokoro voiceover. Duck music to 0.15–0.20 under narration and return to 0.28 in short visual-only moments. Use subtle UI clicks, glass accents and one final logo impact. Verify both music and narration are audible in the rendered MP4.
