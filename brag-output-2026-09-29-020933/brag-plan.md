# Brag Plan: MySchola

## What is this app?
MySchola is an online GCSE tuition platform for Years 9–11, combining live small-group Maths, English and Science lessons with weekly structure, homework, recordings, feedback and clear parent communication.

## The angle
Turn revision anxiety into a visible system of support. The film follows one student from a quiet moment of uncertainty into the MySchola learning loop: choose the right course, join a focused live lesson, revisit the recording, receive feedback, and see progress become something the whole family can understand.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
A black screen resolves into a cool blue MySchola interface fragment and a single line: “GCSE success is built week by week.” The camera moves through the headline into the real homepage UI.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The MySchola homepage: “Close Gaps. Build Confidence. Improve Grades.”
- Real course language for Maths, English Language & Literature, Combined Science and Year 9–11 pathways.
- The timetable as a living weekly rhythm, with colour-coded Maths, English and Science lessons.
- Student dashboard actions: join Zoom sessions, watch recordings and download homework.
- Parent-facing proof: homework, tutor feedback, progress tracking and monthly parent calls.

## Outro / punchline
The whole system contracts into the MySchola mark and the actual CTA: “Book a free consultation.” Supporting line: “No card required.”

## User flow worth showing
Homepage entry → choose a subject / year pathway → weekly timetable → student dashboard → lesson recording and homework feedback → visible progress → free consultation booking.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: cinematic premium edtech startup launch with Apple-style restraint and emotional British voiceover
- Interpretation: Slow, confident camera moves and tactile UI reveals create trust; the edit grows from quiet uncertainty into a warm, energised learning system, with real product copy kept legible and specific.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 60 seconds

- Accent: #2563eb and green #10a56a for action / WhatsApp support
- Text: #111827 and #0d2b52
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body font: ui-sans-serif / system UI stack
- Strongest visual element: the bright, structured blue-white product system: hero progress card, subject cards, timetable calendar and student dashboard panels

## Share copy (draft)
MySchola turns GCSE support into a weekly system students and parents can actually see: live small-group lessons, recordings, feedback and progress in one place.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a clear voice-led arc
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, warm, polished and understated
- Music treatment: 0.18 under narration, rising to 0.30 in visual-only transitions, with a gentle swell into the final CTA and a clean tail
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset at `.agents/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; target major reveals near strong cues around 12s, 31s and 53s, subject to readability
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the deep navy background warmth and soft UI glow breathe with music energy, never use waveform graphics
- SFX posture: sparse and premium; soft UI clicks, light glass / bell accents, one restrained final impact
- Audio-coupled moments: homepage headline reveal, three subject cards, timetable lesson selection, dashboard cards, progress line, final logo / CTA
- Restraint rule: narration and product clarity always win over beat sync or sound design

## Voiceover script

“Every student starts somewhere. A topic they almost understand. A question they keep avoiding. A little less confidence than they had last term.

MySchola turns that uncertainty into a plan. Online GCSE tuition for Years 9, 10 and 11, built around the subjects that matter: Maths, English and Science.

Small-group lessons make space for questions. Tutors explain, challenge and guide. The timetable creates a rhythm that students can return to, week after week.

And learning does not end when the lesson does. Recordings, homework and tutor feedback keep progress moving between sessions.

For parents, the picture stays clear: what is improving, what needs attention, and what comes next.

Because confidence is not a single moment. It is what happens when the right support shows up consistently.

MySchola. Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. Book a free consultation.”
