# MySchola 30-Second Meta Ad - Editorial Revision

## Objective
Create a premium 9:16 parent-focused Meta ad that feels deliberate, cinematic, and unmistakably MySchola. The narration is the edit clock: visuals change only when George introduces a new idea.

## Editorial Rules
- Exactly 30 seconds, 1080x1920.
- Seven chapters, with only six major transitions.
- Use slow camera pushes and one continuous product journey instead of repeated pop-in hooks.
- Keep the brand palette anchored to `#071A33`, `#145DFF`, `#F5F8FD`, white, with red and green only for English and Science.
- Show `FROM £11 PER LESSON` verbatim.
- Keep lesson labels white on solid subject colours.
- Do not show a generic recording title card: show a real recording player with timeline and lesson context.

## Storyboard And Exact Timeline
1. **0.00-3.07 - Parent hook.** Premium pale-blue brand field. A working-grade UI holds while the camera slowly pushes in. George: "Is your child trying, but still falling behind?"
2. **3.07-7.27 - Clarity.** Revision fragments settle into a single clean weekly plan. George: "More revision isn't always the answer. They need a clearer weekly plan."
3. **7.27-12.64 - Product and value.** Reveal the actual MySchola visual system and learning-plan UI. The price badge appears only as George says it. George: "MySchola offers live, small-group GCSE tuition from eleven pounds per lesson."
4. **12.64-16.45 - Subjects and expert tutor.** A slow lateral camera move across Maths, English and Science, ending on the tutor interaction. George: "Maths, English and Science, taught by expert tutors every week."
5. **16.45-20.94 - Connected experience.** One dashboard remains on screen. Lessons, homework, recordings and tutor feedback highlight in spoken order. George: "Lessons, homework, recordings and tutor feedback, connected in one place."
6. **20.94-24.63 - Student and parent journey.** One continuous vertical camera move: timetable, recording player, then progress. George: "They know what's next. They can watch again. You can see progress."
7. **24.63-30.00 - Brand close.** Navy end frame with the supplied logo, line-by-line promise, and CTA. George: "MySchola. Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. Book a free consultation."
