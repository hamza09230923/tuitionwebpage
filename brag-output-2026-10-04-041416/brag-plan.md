# MySchola Meta Ad Plan

## Format
9:16 vertical, 1080 x 1920, 30 seconds maximum.

## Audience and objective
UK parents of GCSE students in Years 9-11. Stop the scroll with a recognisable parent problem, show a structured MySchola solution, prove the real product experience, establish the from GBP11 value, then land the free consultation CTA.

## Creative direction
Premium Apple-inspired product launch adapted for a paid Meta placement. Use a meaningful visual reset every 1.5-2.5 seconds through typography, interface transformations, camera scale, cursor interaction, price reveal, progress animation and beat-led sound design.

## Voiceover
bf_emma, warm professional British delivery. The middle is intentionally condensed so the final brand line can remain natural and exact.

1. Is your child trying, but still falling behind?
2. More revision isn't always the answer.
3. They need a clearer weekly plan.
4. That's MySchola.
5. Live GCSE tuition. Structured every week.
6. Small groups, from eleven pounds a lesson.
7. Maths, English and Science.
8. Lessons, homework, recordings, feedback.
9. One connected weekly routine.
10. Know what's next.
11. Watch it again.
12. See the progress.
13. All in one place.
14. Week by week, it works together.
15. MySchola. Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. Book a free consultation.

## Audio direction
Use the bundled properly cleared music track as a restrained technology pulse at 105-115 BPM. Keep it low under Emma, lift it at the MySchola reveal, price hit and final CTA. Use sparse impact, drop, logo and selection SFX only when they clarify a visual beat.

## Sound-off readability
Kinetic emphasis text carries the story without conventional subtitles: STILL FALLING BEHIND?, A CLEARER WEEKLY PLAN, LIVE GCSE TUITION, FROM GBP11 / LESSON, MATHS / ENGLISH / SCIENCE, KNOW WHAT'S NEXT, WATCH IT AGAIN, PRACTICE - FEEDBACK - PROGRESS, LESS GUESSING / MORE VISIBILITY, and the final brand promise.
