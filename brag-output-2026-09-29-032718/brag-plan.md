# Brag Plan: MySchola

## What is this app?
MySchola is an online GCSE tutoring platform for Years 9-11, combining expert small-group teaching, weekly structure, exam practice and clear parent communication.

## The angle
Turn uncertainty into a clearer next step. The film begins inside the actual MySchola homepage language: an airy pale-blue field, the blue graduation-cap mark, thin orbital lines and floating learning-plan UI. That visual system carries the eye into the homepage, subject system, timetable and student progress path.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An airy MySchola blue field opens with the real learning-plan vocabulary: the logo, a weekly progress card, a selected Maths lesson and a timetable chip. The UI resolves as the narration says every student has potential and needs the right support.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The exact supplied MySchola logo resolves cleanly on a white brand plate, then the real homepage promise and weekly progress card push into frame.
- Maths, English and Science cards arrive one-by-one with the same source palette and copy patterns as the website, then lock into one coherent learning system.
- The live timetable populates across the week, a Maths lesson is selected, then the student dashboard opens to Join Zoom, Recordings, Homework and a visible progress state.

## Outro / punchline
The working UI compresses into the MySchola lockup. The final CTA is quiet and confident: MySchola. Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. Book a free consultation.

## User flow worth showing
Homepage promise -> choose the subject system -> find a live lesson in the weekly timetable -> join/revisit/complete the student journey -> see progress.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: Apple-inspired British edtech product launch with restrained premium motion design
- Interpretation: Wide negative space, deliberate camera pushes, physical UI depth, beat-locked reveals and one continuous transformation keep the film cinematic without adding generic AI imagery.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 30 seconds exactly

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 - The right support - 0-5s
Light homepage-style opening. The supplied MySchola mark sits in the same airy blue environment as the live site. A weekly plan card, selected Maths lesson and timetable chip arrive in sequence while the headline resolves on the left. Keep the motion calm, precise and legible.
Sequential/interaction: yes - pressure words appear as a restrained sequence, then collapse into one progress line.
Audio intent: low cinematic build, voice intimate and clear.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft pencil texture at 0.2s and one low impact as the blue line resolves.
Music: low pulse, building.
Transition mood: dramatic push and light sweep -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 - The reveal - 5-10s
The logo sits on a white brand plate for a beat, then camera pushes through it into the real MySchola homepage language: GCSE Tuition That Helps Students Close Gaps, Build Confidence and Improve Grades. Show the weekly progress card, the Book a Free Consultation CTA and the three subject legend dots. The card slides forward with parallax and its progress bar draws exactly as the narration introduces MySchola.
Sequential/interaction: yes - logo resolves, homepage frame appears, weekly progress bar draws.
Audio intent: clean reveal with one soft glass accent, music opens slightly in the final second.
Audio-coupled idea: logo hit at 5.0s, progress bar begins on the first musical landing.
Transition mood: smooth camera push and match cut -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 - A complete learning system - 10-17s
The homepage plan card stretches into three source-inspired subject cards: Maths, English, Science. They arrive on separate beats, then align into one system. Use exact brand palette: blue Maths, red English, green Science. Each card carries one precise support line and an Exam technique tag, not a wall of copy. A small tutor feedback badge slides into place to support the phrase expert tutors.
Sequential/interaction: yes - Maths, English and Science arrive one-by-one, then snap into a single row.
Audio intent: confident, rhythmic, still voice-forward.
Audio-coupled idea: card-place accents on first and final cards only; no click spam.
Music: rising pulse.
Transition mood: spring-loaded card choreography into a horizontal camera move -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 - Time has a shape - 17-23s
The aligned cards flatten into a real weekly timetable view. Monday through Sunday populate in a single sweep, then the cursor selects Maths - Higher and the selected lesson panel expands. The visible moment must land exactly under small group lessons and complete online learning experience, with the timetable doing the explaining.
Sequential/interaction: yes - lesson cells populate left-to-right, then a single selection expands.
Audio intent: precision and forward motion; voice stays above music.
Audio-coupled idea: one selection click and one low glass confirmation at the panel expansion.
Music: strongest mid-film build.
Transition mood: horizontal wipe driven by the selected row -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 - Progress you can see - 23-27s
The selected lesson becomes the student dashboard. Show only the useful working state: Join Zoom, View recordings, View homework, Tutor feedback, and a progress line moving from Starting point to On track. A small parent update badge appears in the same frame so the final spoken line about supporting every step lands on a clear shared journey.
Sequential/interaction: yes - dashboard cards rise in sequence, progress line draws, parent update badge settles last.
Audio intent: warm lift and emotional resolution.
Audio-coupled idea: staggered card accents, then silence the SFX for the last spoken sentence.
Music: lift, then pull back for CTA.
Transition mood: soft compression into the final logo -> Scene 6

### Scene 6 - Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. - 24-30s
The dashboard collapses into the MySchola logo lockup on a white field framed by the brand navy and blue. The voice lands exactly on: MySchola. Close gaps. Build confidence. Improve grades. Book a free consultation. Keep the centered mark, wordmark, headline, CTA and No card required hold clean and readable.
Sequential/interaction: yes - logo, tagline, CTA and underline land in that order.
Audio intent: one premium final impact, then a clean tail.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo impact at 28.2s; music fades under the last word.
Music: brief lift, then fade.
Transition mood: clean final hold.
